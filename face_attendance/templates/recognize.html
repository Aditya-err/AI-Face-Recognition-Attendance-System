    How it works

    1. Faces are detected in every processed frame (fast).
    2. Face embeddings are extracted only when needed - never on every frame.
    3. Embeddings are compared to every registered person's stored embeddings.
    4. A green match records attendance; amber means "uncertain"; red is unknown.
